2. What we provide

Unfollow Finder reads two files from the data export Instagram gives you: followers_1.json and following.json and compares them.

  • Free: the comparison runs in your browser. It shows who doesn't follow you back, who follows you that you don't follow back, and your mutuals.
  • Pro: we store the usernames from each scan so the next one can be compared against it, which is what makes unfollow detection possible.

We never ask for your Instagram password and never connect to your Instagram account.

9. About accuracy

The comparison itself is exact: if a username is in one file and not the other, we report it correctly.

Unfollow detection is a comparison, not a notification. Instagram never tells anyone who unfollowed them. We work it out by noticing that a username has disappeared since your last scan which also happens if that person deactivated their account, changed their username, or was suspended.

Your export is also a snapshot from the moment Instagram generated it, which can be hours before you upload it. We provide the tool as it is and don't guarantee that every result reflects a deliberate unfollow.
